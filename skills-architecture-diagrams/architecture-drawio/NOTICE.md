# Provenance

This skill is MIT. The files below come from third parties and keep their original licenses.

- `scripts/shapesearch.py`, `scripts/validate.py`, `scripts/edgeports.py` — unmodified copies
  of Agents365-ai's [drawio-skill](https://github.com/Agents365-ai/drawio-skill),
  MIT. Text in `licenses/MIT-drawio-skill.txt`.
- `data/shape-index.json.gz` — shape index from
  [jgraph/drawio-mcp](https://github.com/jgraph/drawio-mcp), generated from the official libraries of the
  draw.io client, Apache License 2.0. Text in `licenses/Apache-2.0.txt`. Modification
  applied: compression with `gzip -9`; content unchanged.

No icon package is redistributed. The shapes are resolved by style name; the
drawings themselves are rendered by the draw.io installed on the machine.
